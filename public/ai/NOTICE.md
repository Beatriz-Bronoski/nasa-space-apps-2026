The bundled graph and weights in coco-ssd/ are TensorFlow's SSD Lite MobileNet V2 object detector. Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/tfjs-models/savedmodel/ssdlite_mobilenet_v2/model.json and the five shards named in its manifest. TensorFlow Models and the TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD wrapper are distributed under Apache License 2.0. See LICENSE and https://github.com/tensorflow/tfjs-models/tree/master/coco-ssd. TensorFlow.js 4.22.0 and COCO-SSD 2.2.3 browser bundles are copied from the pinned npm packages at build or dev startup. Inference reads all assets from the same origin.
The optional on-device cat/dog check uses TensorFlow's ImageNet MobileNet V1 alpha 0.50 (224 pixel input), published at https://storage.googleapis.com/tfjs-models/tfjs/mobilenet_v1_0.50_224/model.json. The original shards are concatenated in manifest order into mobilenet/weights.bin. The topology and tensor values are unchanged.
